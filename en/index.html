Alveolar bone grafting

This site is about alveolar bone grafting for a cleft lip and palate. Alveolar bone grafting is an operation. It puts new bone into a gap in the bone of the upper gum.

There are 3 models. Each one shows a stage of care, in order. Tap a stage to see its model and read about it. Start with stage 1.

Choose a stage

1Pre-op: before the bone graftAge about 8 to 10 2Short-term post-op: soon after the bone graftAge about 9 to 11 3Long-term post-op: years later, after bracesAge about 15 to 18

Every person is different

The size of the gap, the teeth and the plan can be very different from person to person. So the facts here are general. They are not advice for one person. Before any treatment, the cleft team will talk with each person and family in detail about the benefits, risks and choices.
