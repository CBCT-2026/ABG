Every person is different

The size of the gap, the teeth and the plan can be very different from person to person. So the facts here are general. They are not advice for one person. Before any treatment, the cleft team will talk with each person and family in detail about the benefits, risks and choices.

Years after the bone graft

This model shows the mouth in the teen years. All the adult teeth have come through, except the wisdom teeth. The bone graft has joined with the bone around it. The adult canine came through the new bone.1

Braces

Most people with a cleft lip and palate have full braces once the adult teeth are in.2 Braces are small parts glued to the teeth, joined by a wire. They slowly move the teeth.

If the lateral incisor is missing, braces can move the canine forward into its space. This closes the gap.23 The tooth behind the canine, the first premolar, then takes the place of the canine.4 This model shows the teeth after the braces have come off.

Making the canine look like a side tooth

A canine is longer and more pointed than a lateral incisor. So the dentist can change its shape to look more like a lateral incisor:54

  • smooth down the point of the tooth
  • add a tooth-coloured filling, called composite
  • if more change is needed, put a thin cover (a veneer) or a crown on the tooth

In this model, the crown is a part that can be taken off. This shows the canine under it.

Other choices: a new tooth in the gap

Instead of closing the gap, braces can keep the space open, with the canine in its own place. A false tooth then fills the space of the lateral incisor.5 The cleft team picks the best choice for each person. It depends on the space and on the teeth next to the gap.5 The model has a part for each choice.

Dental implant. A small post is put into the bone, and a false tooth (a crown) is fixed on top.5 A review found that about 96 in 100 implant teeth were still in place after 5 years.5 An implant is most often put in at about 18 to 21 years of age, when growth has slowed.6 In the model, the implant crown can be taken off to show the post.

Bridge. A false tooth is held by a thin wing. The wing is glued to the back of the tooth next to the gap, often the front tooth.7 This type is called a cantilever resin-bonded bridge. It needs little or no drilling of the teeth. A review found it had fewer problems than a bridge glued to the teeth on both sides.8 In one large study, about 97 in 100 were still in place after 15 years.9 It can be used when an implant is not possible.5

Both closing the gap and an implant can look good. A review found no clear difference in looks between them.10

Other care in the teen years

Some people also have an operation on the jaws, or on the lip or nose. In one Australian hospital, jaw operations were done at an average age of about 16.11

Questions to ask the cleft team

  • Is it better to close the gap or to keep the space?
  • What can be done to change the shape of the canine?
  • Is a crown, an implant or a bridge best, and when can it be done?
  • What care is needed after the braces come off?
