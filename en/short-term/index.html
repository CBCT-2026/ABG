This model shows the same jaw about 3 months after the bone graft. New bone fills the gap, and the gum has healed over it.

What has changed

The graft fills the gap from the floor of the nose down to the gum. It joins the two parts of the upper jaw.12

The surgeon closed the gum over the graft with stitches. If the wound opens, you can lose some of the graft. Surgeons close the gum with care, because a wound that opens is the main problem after this operation.34

The eye tooth is still in the bone. It is a little lower than before. Over the next months it moves down toward the graft.56

Checks after surgery

You will have an X-ray at about 3 to 6 months. It shows how well the new bone has taken.78 Some of the graft shrinks over time. Teeth that move into the graft help keep the bone.910

The hip

Your hip can be sore for a while after the surgeon takes the bone. In one study, children went back to school about 2 weeks after surgery.11
