The three stages3つの段階

  1. 1Before surgery: the gap in the bone and the eye tooth waiting to come down
    手術の前：骨のすき間と、おりてくるのを待つ犬歯
  2. 2About 3 months after: new bone fills the gap
    約3か月後：新しい骨がすき間をうめる
  3. 3Years later: the eye tooth is through, and braces line up the teeth
    数年後：犬歯が出てきて、そうちで歯をならべる
