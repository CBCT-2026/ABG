What has changed

Bone now joins the two parts of the jaw where the gap was.[[bergland]]

The eye tooth came down through the graft. It is now fully in the row of teeth.[[rivers]][[vellone]]

Most eye teeth come through on their own. Some get stuck. Then a small operation uncovers the tooth, and the braces slowly pull it down.[[vellone]][[hoang]][[parkin]]

Fixed braces line up the teeth. Almost everyone who has this graft needs them.[[bergland]][[semb]]

The missing tooth

There are two main plans for the gap left by the tooth that never grew.

Close the space. Braces move the eye tooth into the place of the missing tooth. Then the dentist reshapes it and adds tooth-coloured filling so it looks like the missing tooth. Teams use this plan most often for children with a cleft.[[lee2024]][[bergland]][[kokich]][[zachrisson]]

Keep the space. While you are still growing, a plate you can take out holds a false tooth in the gap. When you stop growing, your dentist can fill the gap for good with:[[clerc]][[knobloch]]

  • an implant: a small post in the bone with a false tooth (a crown) on top
  • a bridge: a false tooth with one wing glued to the back of the tooth next to the gap

The model has parts you can swap to show each choice. The braces clip on and off.

Your cleft team will talk with you about which plan suits you.[[clerc]]
