This model shows the same jaw in the later teens, after the braces come off. Your dentist can now fill the gap where the small tooth never grew.

What has changed

The braces are off. Bone holds the teeth where the gap was.[[bergland]][[jahanbin]]

You have stopped growing, so your dentist can now fill the gap for good.[[clerc]][[knobloch]]

Ways to fill the gap

There are three main ways:[[clerc]]

  • an implant: a small post in the bone with a false tooth (a crown) on top[[knobloch]]
  • a bridge: a false tooth with one wing glued to the back of the tooth next to the gap
  • a plate you can take out (a partial denture) that holds a false tooth

The model has parts you can swap to show each choice.

Some teams close the space instead. Braces move the eye tooth into the place of the missing tooth. Then the dentist reshapes it so it looks like the missing tooth. Teams use this plan most often for children with a cleft. The model does not show this plan.[[lee2024]][[bergland]][[kokich]][[zachrisson]]

Your cleft team will talk with you about which plan suits you.[[clerc]]
