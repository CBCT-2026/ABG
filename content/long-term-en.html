Every person is different

The size of the gap, the teeth and the plan can be very different from person to person. So the facts here are general. They are not advice for one person. Before any treatment, the cleft team will talk with each person and family in detail about the benefits, risks and choices.

Years after the bone graft

This model shows the mouth in the teen years. All the adult teeth have come through, except the wisdom teeth. The bone graft has joined with the bone around it. The adult canine came through the new bone.[[jahanbin]]

Braces

Most people with a cleft lip and palate have full braces once the adult teeth are in.[[freitas]] Braces are small parts glued to the teeth, joined by a wire. They slowly move the teeth.

If the lateral incisor is missing, braces can move the canine forward into its space. This closes the gap.[[freitas]][[cassolato]] The tooth behind the canine, the first premolar, then takes the place of the canine.[[manfio]] This model shows the teeth after the braces have come off.

Making the canine look like a side tooth

A canine is longer and more pointed than a lateral incisor. So the dentist can change its shape to look more like a lateral incisor:[[clerc]][[manfio]]

  • smooth down the point of the tooth
  • add a tooth-coloured filling, called composite
  • if more change is needed, put a thin cover (a veneer) or a crown on the tooth

In this model, the crown is a part that can be taken off. This shows the canine under it.

Another choice: a new tooth in the gap

Instead of closing the gap, braces can keep the space open. A false tooth then fills it. It can be held by a dental implant (a small post in the bone) or by a bridge glued to the teeth next to it.[[clerc]] A review found that about 96 in 100 implant teeth were still in place after 5 years.[[clerc]] An implant is most often put in at about 18 to 21 years of age, when growth has slowed.[[robbins]]

Both choices can look good. A review found no clear difference in looks between closing the gap and an implant.[[migliorati]] The cleft team picks the best choice for each person. It depends on the space and on the teeth next to the gap.[[clerc]]

Other care in the teen years

Some people also have an operation on the jaws, or on the lip or nose. In one Australian hospital, jaw operations were done at an average age of about 16.[[jensen]]

Questions to ask the cleft team

  • Is it better to close the gap or to keep the space?
  • What can be done to change the shape of the canine?
  • When can a crown, veneer or implant be done?
  • What care is needed after the braces come off?
