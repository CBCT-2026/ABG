This model shows the same jaw about 2 to 3 years after the bone graft. The child is about 12 to 13 years old. The eye tooth has come down through the new bone. Fixed braces line up the teeth.

What has changed

Bone now joins the two parts of the upper jaw where the gap was.[[bergland]][[semb]]

The eye tooth came down through the graft into the row of teeth.[[rivers]][[vellone]]

Most eye teeth come through on their own. Some get stuck. Then a small operation uncovers the tooth, and the braces slowly pull it down.[[vellone]][[hoang]][[parkin]]

Some of the graft shrinks over time. Teeth that move into the graft help keep the bone.[[jahanbin]][[schultze]]

The braces

Fixed braces line up the teeth. Almost everyone who has this graft needs them.[[bergland]][[semb]]

A false tooth on the brace wire fills the gap where the small tooth never grew. It holds the space until the braces come off.

The braces on the model clip on and off.

After the operation

In the first weeks, the gum heals over the graft. If the wound opens, you can lose some of the graft.[[siddiqui]][[kaura]]

An X-ray at about 3 to 6 months shows how well the new bone has taken.[[kindelan]][[witherow]]

Your hip can be sore for a while after the surgeon takes the bone. In one study, children went back to school about 2 weeks after surgery.[[tache]]
